The Atlas of the Sound of the Body is a research project on how cultures across history have made the sound of the human body visible — as a line. This installation presents five pairs of images, each bringing together two very different ways of drawing that sound: a medieval chant notation next to a modern heart tracing, a sacred Balinese figure next to a hospital chart. Singer Arthur Wilkens looked at each pair and turned what he saw into voice. Click a pair to read more about it, and use the three buttons below each one to listen: left image, right image, or both mixed together.
